One UI/controller integration surface. Resolution reads summaries only; opening one file issues native tokens for its concrete commit. The controller projects resolved files to that owner for existing drop validation. Ambiguous, immutable, missing/merge/conflicted/divergent or bounded-out comparisons remain read-only with guidance. All provider mutations remain guarded by the existing backend. Unit and cold application-shell Storybook evidence qualify this frontend slice; real-storage/provider/platform/release and visual-baseline gates are unchanged. Browser interaction through the desktop tool was disabled in Work settings and was not retried; deterministic headless tests were used. Root aggregates, Rust lanes, contract generation and full unrelated suites are out of scope under targeted policy.
